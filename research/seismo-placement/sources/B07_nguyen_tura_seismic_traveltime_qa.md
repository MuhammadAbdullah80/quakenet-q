## Verbatim quotes
1. "The seismic inversion problem is first converted into a Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization problem, which the quantum annealer is specifically designed to solve."
2. "in our survey, 20 pairs of sources and receivers are non-uniformly deployed within two boreholes"
3. "The sources and receivers of non-uniform placement are distributed according to a quadratic polynomial distribution."

## Relevance
Not placement optimisation: geometry is a fixed hand-chosen quadratic spacing; the QUBO is velocity inversion. Shows seismology + annealing exists while station siting is not what is optimised. Authors say the method "can be applied to ... earthquake or micro seismic data".
