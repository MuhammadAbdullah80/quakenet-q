## Verbatim quotes

3. "All of these properties even hold for Linear Nearest Neighbor architectures."

## Relevance
Initial state for exact-k QAOA: equal superposition of all n-bit strings of Hamming weight k, O(kn) gates, depth O(n), no ancillas. Cheap for a few tens of candidate sites.
