When the next earthquake happens

What the QuakeNet-Q agent does, in order, and which algorithm runs at each step. Click a step to highlight what it passes data to. Click the background to reset.

Wake up
Minute 0
Earthquake detectedFeed pollingThe agent checks the USGS live feed every minute. A magnitude 6.5+ quake on land starts a run.→ passes on: quake id, location, time
Minutes 1–5
Gather the factsData downloadDownloads the aftershocks so far (USGS) and the permanent sensors nearby (EarthScope). Loads what it learned from past earthquakes.→ passes on: aftershock list, station list, learned settings
Plan
First hours
Lay out candidate sites and score themD-criterion (classical)Draws a grid of possible sites around the aftershock zone. For each site: how much it sharpens aftershock locations, how much it duplicates other sites, how risky it is to reach.→ passes on: a score for every site, pair, triple and group of four
Quantum circuit chooses 4 sitesDicke state + XY-mixer QAOA (Qiskit)Starts with every valid 4-site network at once, then repeatedly marks good networks and shuffles between valid ones. Starts from circuit settings that worked on past earthquakes; an optimiser fine-tunes them.→ passes on: about 1,000 measured networks, best ones appearing most often
Self-checkExact re-scoring + classical searchRe-scores the measured networks with the full seismology score and compares with a classical search. Agree → publish. Disagree → more circuit layers and more shots, then check again.agree → publish · disagree → retry quantum step
Plan ready
Publish the site planReportA map with the 4 chosen sites, coordinates, expected accuracy, risk and a confidence level.→ passes on: plan to field teams; expected accuracy to the monitor
Watch and improve
Every few hours
Watch the new aftershocksMonitoringDownloads the newest aftershocks and checks how accurately they are being located, against what the agent predicted.→ passes on: predicted vs. actual accuracy, where the aftershocks are now
Has reality drifted from the plan?Deviation testIf accuracy is much worse than predicted, or the aftershocks have moved, the agent corrects its model and re-plans.no → keep watching · yes → re-plan
Re-plan with a moving costQAOA again (Qiskit)Runs the quantum step on the updated data. Moving a sensor costs something, so it only recommends a move that is worth it.→ passes on: "move sensor from site A to site B", or "stay"
Remember
After the sequence
Save what it learnedCalibration + angle libraryStores the corrected wave speed and timing error, the circuit settings that worked, and how good its predictions were. The next earthquake starts from here.→ feeds: the next run's facts and quantum step
