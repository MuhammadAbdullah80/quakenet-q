Problem statement

After a major earthquake, response teams have hours to decide where a handful of portable seismometers go. Good placement sharpens aftershock locations, which feeds hazard maps and rescue decisions, but published deployments describe siting by expert judgement and simple rules. We formulate siting as a budget-limited optimisation that balances location accuracy, station redundancy and access risk. We solve it with a constraint-preserving hybrid QAOA and check it against exact and classical methods on the real 2019 Ridgecrest sequence.
